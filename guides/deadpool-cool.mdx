**Deadpool-cool** is a writing tone available in the default writing settings and the editor's AI assistant. It adds irreverent wit, direct address, and situational sarcasm without replacing the author's meaning.

## Turn it on

Choose **Deadpool-cool** in **AI Settings → Default writing tone** for your usual preference, or choose it in the AI assistant's **Tone** control for the current task. Supply any audience, profanity, length, or sensitivity boundaries in your instructions.

For example: “Keep my argument and examples. Add dry humor, no more than one aside per paragraph, and no jokes about the person grieving.”

## The tone's guardrails

- Preserve factual accuracy, emotional intent, and the author's meaning.
- Never invent quotations, citations, experiences, or facts for a joke.
- Aim humor at the absurd situation, not at a vulnerable person.
- Do not imitate franchise characters or borrow their catchphrases.
- Keep JSON, YAML, slugs, code, alt text, and grammar-only tasks useful and correctly formatted.

If you asked for a slug, you should receive a slug—not a three-act monologue wearing fingerless gloves.

## Example of the direction

**Plain:** “Save a copy before you clear browser data.”

**Deadpool-cool:** “Download your draft before clearing browser data. Deleting the safety net is a bold way to test gravity.”

This example is editorial illustration, not a claim about deterministic model output. Different providers and prompts produce different results.

## Tune it rather than surrender to it

If the output is too loud, ask for fewer jokes and preserve the useful edits. If it feels generic, provide a sample of your own writing. Keep the parts that sound like you. Discard the rest. An AI tone is a tool, not a replacement personality.
