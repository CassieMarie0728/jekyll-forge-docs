The editor works with a Markdown body and YAML front matter. Your Jekyll theme determines how the finished site renders that content. Forge's preview helps you edit; it does not run your complete production theme and plugin pipeline.

## A minimal post

```markdown
---
title: "A little less chaos"
layout: post
date: 2026-09-20
tags:
  - writing
  - jekyll
---

## The part worth saying

Your words go here. **Emphasis** is optional. Meaning is not.
```

Use the layout your theme actually provides. `post` is an example, not a universal Jekyll law. Required fields, categories, excerpt behavior, image fields, and custom metadata vary by theme.

## Formatting without placeholder debris

Heading controls format the current line and replace existing heading markers. They should not insert labels such as “Heading 1” into your prose. List formatting preserves selected content; empty inline formatting places the caret inside its markers.

Use one top-level document title according to your theme's conventions. Many themes already display the front-matter title, so a duplicate body H1 may be unnecessary.

## Metadata that survives a round trip

Forge parses and serializes YAML with a YAML library. Nested values, multiline strings, quoted text, and backslashes are supported. Malformed YAML is rejected instead of silently published. Parsing and serialization can normalize formatting; comments are not a dependable place to keep information you need preserved.

When editing structured metadata, keep arrays as arrays and nested objects as objects. If a field contains a date-like string that must remain a string, quote it and verify the serialized output before publication.

## Preview versus production

Check headings, links, lists, and ordinary Markdown in the editor. Check Liquid templates, custom plugins, theme layouts, and final image URLs in the actual Jekyll build. A local preview looking fine cannot rescue a missing gem in your deployment.

## Moving between posts

Save or download your work before switching posts. New Post can ask before discarding unsaved edits; refresh and close also warn. Browser recovery provides another route back, but warnings and recovery are not a substitute for saving important work.
